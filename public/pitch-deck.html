Needs

  • Validate ideas & gain early visibility

  • Access mentors, incubators & investors
  • Showcase progress & attend events

Where We Find Them

  • LinkedIn, AngelList, YC Startup School

Business Model · Facilitator Marketplace

PARTNERS, not CUSTOMERS.

FACILITATOR KEEPS 60–70%

Facilitators onboard their cohort free and get a dashboard to manage them. Revenue comes from the startup marketplace, not subscriptions.

Marketplace Access
Corporates, sponsors & hiring companies pay to access a cohort (e.g. 30 startups for ₹15k).
Revenue Share
Ments takes 30–40%; facilitator keeps 60–70% as incentive to grow their cohort.
Analytics Plan (at scale)
Optional ₹2,999/mo for advanced cohort analytics & portfolio reporting.
